# Brand Guidelines

## Brand essence

Secret Sip Feed is playful, trend-aware, and creator-friendly.

## Voice and tone

- Fun and energetic
- Clear and practical
- Inclusive and non-judgmental

## Visual direction

- Gradient-heavy pastel palette (pink/purple/indigo)
- Rounded cards, soft shadows, high-contrast CTAs
- Emoji-friendly headings for social-native context

## UX principles

- Keep discovery quick (few clicks to a recipe)
- Keep actions obvious (favorite/import/create)
- Keep trust visible (clear auth state and error messaging)

## Messaging pillars

1. Discover what is trending now
2. Save and organize your custom sips
3. Import social recipes with less friction
